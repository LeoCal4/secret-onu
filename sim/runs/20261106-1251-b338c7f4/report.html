Assumptions the rules don't define
  • Sections: low 0–6, medium 7–13, high 14–20.
  • Costs: events 6/8/10/12, votes 7/9/11/13 for 3/4/5/6 players. Loss at the 5th unhandled event.
  • Hand: one card each of 0–5. Each player plays one card on the event and one on the vote every round.
  • Vote deck: 40 random cards, each moving two quadrants by 1 step (70%) or 2 steps (30%), in a random direction.
  • Each player has one objective and all objectives at the table are different.
  • The President picks the card that best serves their own objective. In the lying runs, they claim the opposite effect whenever the card hurts them.
  • Bots estimate each decision by how far their objective would be from its target section after the remaining rounds. They do not plan further or read other players.
